## Caveats

- Regret against a truth table is measured from the table's own best option, whose estimate is biased upward (the maximum of noisy means); differences between two picks on one table (section 2, budget) do not carry that bias.
- Section 2's interval on a difference treats the two options' truth intervals as independent; the truth plays them on common seeds, so the real interval is narrower and `unclear` is conservative.
- The margin-rule variant is an approximation through the win scale; the LCB rule (D350) needs the paired per-round differences, which the rankers do not report, and is not simulated.
- `review-leaf` regrets are end-of-turn estimates on the fitted leaf scale (D349), not win rates.
- Card credit is shared: a decision's regret is counted once per card it names, so card totals overlap.
